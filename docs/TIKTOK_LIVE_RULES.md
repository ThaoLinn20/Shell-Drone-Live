## 3. Quyen Loi Dac Biet Cua Nguoi Xem Livestream

1. Nhan vat 3D rieng tren bai bien:
   - Khi ban comment, tha tim hoac tang qua, mot nhan vat 3D tren bai bien Atami se mang ten TikTok cua ban.
   - Nhan vat se reo ho, hien thi bong thoai noi chuyen va phat am thanh nguoi noi tieng Viet song dong.

2. Ngoi vi VIP Lounge Danh du (Top Gifter):
   - Nguoi xem co tong diem xu cao nhat phong Live se duoc he thong dua len ngoi o vi tri dem vang trung tam cua khu vuc VIP Lounge mat bien.

3. Xoay tua phien ban thong minh (Consecutive Version Rotation):
   - Khi tang lien tiep cac mon qua cung chu de trong 60 giay (vi du qua 100 xu), he thong tu dong phat tiep cac phan tiep theo: Part 1 -> Part 2 -> Part 3 -> Part 4 (Grand Finale) ma khong bi trung lap doan dau.

4. Hang doi uu tien kim cuong (Priority Queue):
   - Neu show truoc dang ban, qua cua ban se duoc dua vao hang doi uu tien.
   - Qua co gia tri xu cao hon se luon duoc he thong xep len dau de ban truoc ngay khi man phao hoa hien tai ket thuc.
